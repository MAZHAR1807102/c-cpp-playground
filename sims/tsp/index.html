The travelling salesman problem, step by step

Find the cheapest tour that starts at city 1, visits every other city exactly once and returns to city 1. The problem is NP-hard: the exact methods below all take exponential time in the worst case, so for large inputs we use heuristics and approximation algorithms instead. Compare them on the same cities.
