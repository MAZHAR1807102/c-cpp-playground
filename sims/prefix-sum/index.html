Prefix and suffix sums, step by step

Spend Θ(n) once to store running totals, then answer “what is the sum of this range?” in O(1). A prefix sum P[i] totals everything before i; a suffix sum S[i] totals everything from i to the end. The same idea, with max or product in place of +, solves many array problems in one or two passes.
