Algorithms · Game theory

Game theory algorithms, step by step

How should a rational player choose when the outcome also depends on an opponent? For game trees (chess, tic-tac-toe) use minimax and its faster version alpha–beta. For matrix games look for saddle points, mixed strategies, dominated strategies and Nash equilibria. For Nim a single XOR decides who wins.
